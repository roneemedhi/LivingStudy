# LivingStudy — director's treatment

**Promise:** Your own notes become questions you can trust, one deliberate approval at a time.  
**Identity:** Quietly vivid study-room palette: deep ink green, warm paper, sage, and a small persimmon signal; Fraunces display paired with DM Sans and DM Mono.  
**Hero:** A sentence fragment, physically carried through a review gate into a searchable verified library and then recalled.  
**Motion:** Type cutouts, camera-pushed interface choreography, and a sentence-to-card graphic match.  
**Arc:** A sharp question mark becomes a cascade of source text, then slows at the human approval moment, opens into practice, and resolves in a quiet lockup.  
**Payoff:** The final library cards fold into one paper leaf mark; the opening's question-mark dot becomes the leaf's seed, closing a visual loop.  
**Format:** 16:9 landscape.  
**Runtime:** 25 seconds across five 5-second scenes.
